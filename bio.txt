Lekan Adeyeri is a computer science and physics undergraduate on leave from Pitzer College within the Claremont Colleges. His work sits between computer vision, deep learning, 3D modelling, neural surrogates for physical simulation, and tools that make technical creation more accessible and sustainable. He has contributed to Stanford-led research on deep-learning enhancement of ultra-low-dose PET/MRI, worked on a GPT-3-powered chatbot and AI tooling during a Microsoft internship, and built production-oriented software systems across research, health technology, and developer tools. He is especially interested in learned representations that make physical and geometric processes smaller, more inspectable, and more efficient to run.
